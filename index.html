How to Play

  • 🎣 Catch — set fruit bait in your lure slots and wait (skip with 🎟️). When a bug appears, tap it and stop the marker in the green — hit the sweet spot for a bigger catch. Sparkling bugs are rare & carry a talent.
  • 🎰 Capsule — spend ✨ Glimmer (earned in battle) to pull a random bug. Transparent odds + pity (guaranteed Rare/Epic).
  • 🐛 Bugs — build a team. Talents buff the team and give each bug a battle ability.
  • ⚔️ Battle — tap to build a combo, fire team abilities, and unleash the SWARM ultimate. Read enemy modifiers and pick your tactic. Beat the boss to unlock the next area.
  • ⚗️ Lab — merge two of the same bug into a bigger, stronger one. 🛒 Shop — spend 🎟️ on upgrades (more lure slots, idle power, catch assist…). 🎯 Goals for bonus rewards.
