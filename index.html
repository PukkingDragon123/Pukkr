How to Play

  • 🎣 Catch — set fruit bait and wait for a bug (skip the wait with 🎟️ tokens). Rarer bugs sparkle and have a talent. Tap the bug, then beat the shrinking catch bar.
  • 🐛 Bugs — build a team of 3. Talents buff the whole team (Mighty/Lucky/Healer/Tough/Swift), so mix wisely!
  • ⚔️ Battle — tap to attack (your team also fights on its own). Clear waves, beat the boss to unlock the next area, and earn tokens.
  • ⚗️ Lab — merge two of the same bug into a bigger, stronger one.
  • Use the ◀ ▶ at the top to travel between unlocked areas. Esc closes a window.
