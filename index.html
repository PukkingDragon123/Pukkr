How to Play

  • ⛩️ Forest — enter the shrine and pick a path. You'll find a bug spot, candy, food, or a charm game. In a bug spot, tap a fast-moving creature, then stop the shrinking catch bar in the green!
  • 🎒 Backpack — caught creatures take up space by size on a grid. Drag to tidy them; upgrade the bag in the Shop.
  • 🌷 Garden — drag food onto a jar to grow your creature. Bigger = worth more candy! Upgrade the garden to grow faster.
  • 🏛 Museum — display grown creatures, then open the doors for visiting friends and candy.
  • 🛒 Shop — buy a better net, a bigger backpack and food.
  • Switch places with the bottom bar. Esc closes a window; H shows this help.
