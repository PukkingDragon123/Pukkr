How to Play

  • Move with WASD or the arrow keys.
  • Interact / Swing net with Space, E or Enter. Walk up to a wandering bug and swing!
  • Time the catch meter — stop the marker in the green zone. Better nets make the zone bigger.
  • Open menus: C Bugdex · J Jar · R Terrarium · M Museum · B Shop.
  • Raise bugs in your terrarium at home — they grow happier and drip candy.
  • Donate bugs to the Museum. Friends visit and reward you with candy and praise.
  • Spend candy at the Shop to upgrade your net, shoes, lures and more.
  • Different bugs come out at different times of day and in different places. Explore!
  • Your game auto-saves. Press Esc to close any window — and H any time to see this help again.
  • On a phone or tablet? On-screen controls appear automatically. 🐛
