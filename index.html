How to Play

  • Catch — click or tap any creature drifting through a scene to swing your net. (Or press Space to net the nearest one.)
  • Time the catch meter — stop the marker in the green zone. Better nets make the zone bigger; rare creatures are trickier.
  • Travel between the Garden and the Woods with the bottom bar (or 1/2). Different creatures appear in each place and at different times of day.
  • Raise caught creatures in your Jars — feed them to raise their Love, earn candy, and watch them evolve.
  • Donate creatures to the Museum, then open the doors so friends visit and reward you with candy.
  • Spend candy at the Shop on better nets, jars, lures and food.
  • Menus: C Bugdex · J Satchel · R Jars · M Museum · B Shop.
  • Your game auto-saves. Press Esc to close a window — and H any time to see this help again.
