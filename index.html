How to Play

  • 🌳 Forest — click or tap a creature drifting by to net it. Time the meter into the green zone.
  • 🌷 Garden — your creatures live here in jars. Tap a jar to feed it (with candy) and raise its Love; happy creatures drip candy back.
  • 🏛 Museum — from a Garden jar, choose Display in Museum. Then open the doors and friends visit with candy — more when an exhibit matches the habitat they love.
  • Switch places with the bottom bar (or 1/2/3). There's a gentle day/night cycle — fireflies come out at night.
  • Your game auto-saves. Esc closes a window; H shows this help.
